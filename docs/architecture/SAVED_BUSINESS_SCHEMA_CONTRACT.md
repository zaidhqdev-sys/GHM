# GHM Saved Business Schema Contract

**Status:** CONSTRUCTION CONTRACT — 2026-09-16

## 1. Purpose

Define the provider-neutral persistence boundary for the Saved Business capability reconciled from the production Zaid Connect source audit.

This document authorizes only the Saved Business relationship resource. It does not construct or modify Business Identity, verification, Trust, membership, profile, ranking, notification, or other capabilities.

## 2. Canonical resource

Resource name:

```text
ghm.saved_business
```

A Saved Business row represents one authenticated GHM account saving one GHM Business.

The owning account is the GHM account identity represented by `ghm.account_identity(id)`. The target is the canonical GHM Business represented by `ghm.business(id)`.

This is a relationship resource, not a second Business representation.

## 3. Schema

The construction schema is:

```sql
CREATE TABLE ghm.saved_business (
  id bigint GENERATED BY DEFAULT AS IDENTITY PRIMARY KEY,
  account_id bigint NOT NULL
    REFERENCES ghm.account_identity(id)
    ON DELETE CASCADE,
  business_id bigint NOT NULL
    REFERENCES ghm.business(id)
    ON DELETE CASCADE,
  created_at timestamptz NOT NULL DEFAULT now(),

  CONSTRAINT saved_business_account_business_unique
    UNIQUE (account_id, business_id)
);
```

No `updated_at` column is authorized because the production relationship has no arbitrary UPDATE operation.

No provider-specific user identifier is stored.

## 4. Ownership

`account_id` is the owner of the saved relationship.

The application authentication boundary supplies the authenticated account through `AuthContext`. Callers must not be able to choose or spoof an arbitrary owner account identifier.

The repository/service boundary must bind Saved Business reads and mutations to `context.userId`.

The target `business_id` is the business-side input. It must resolve to the canonical `ghm.business` resource.

## 5. Uniqueness

The pair `(account_id, business_id)` is unique.

Therefore one account cannot persist duplicate Saved Business relationships for the same Business.

Duplicate creation must be treated as a governed domain outcome rather than creating a second relationship row.

## 6. Target Business eligibility

A Saved Business may be created only when the target Business satisfies the reconciled public Business condition:

```text
is_active = true
AND is_verified = true
AND verification_status = 'approved'
```

The `is_verified` and `verification_status` relationship is already constrained by the Business Identity reconciliation. Saved Business does not own or mutate those fields.

No save may be created for an inactive, unverified, pending/under-review, rejected, or otherwise non-approved Business.

## 7. Operations

The initial resource surface is exactly:

```text
read
create
delete
```

There is no arbitrary UPDATE operation.

The product may present create/delete as a toggle, but GHM exposes the underlying operations explicitly.

There is no administrator Saved Business mutation surface authorized by this contract.

There is no anonymous operation authorized by this contract.

## 8. Read boundary

An authenticated account may read only its own Saved Business relationships.

The canonical ownership predicate is:

```text
saved_business.account_id = context.userId
```

A read must not expose another account's saved relationships.

When a Saved Business read returns Business data, the service must use the canonical public Business projection. Private Business registration/review workflow data and private account/profile data are outside this resource's read contract.

## 9. Create boundary

Create requires:

1. an authenticated GHM account;
2. the authenticated account to be the persisted `account_id`;
3. a canonical target `ghm.business`;
4. the target Business to satisfy the public eligibility condition;
5. the `(account_id, business_id)` pair not already to exist.

The caller supplies the target Business identifier, not the owner account identifier.

## 10. Delete boundary

Delete is owner-scoped.

A Saved Business may be deleted only when its `account_id` matches the authenticated account represented by `AuthContext`.

Cross-account deletion is denied.

Business deletion is not authorized by this resource; the Business foreign key only establishes relationship lifecycle through governed referential integrity.

## 11. Runtime privilege direction

The runtime database role must receive only the privileges required by the finalized repository/service implementation.

The contract does not authorize blanket table DML.

In particular:

- SELECT is required for the authorized read surface;
- INSERT is required for create;
- DELETE is required for delete;
- UPDATE is not required and must not be granted;
- sequence usage is required if runtime performs identity-backed INSERTs directly;
- cleanup authority remains separate from runtime authority.

Exact grants are finalized in the migration together with qualification evidence. Any mutation path that bypasses the authenticated application boundary is out of contract.

## 12. Referential integrity

Both relationships use `ON DELETE CASCADE`:

```text
saved_business.account_id  -> ghm.account_identity.id
saved_business.business_id -> ghm.business.id
```

This mirrors the production relationship lifecycle while keeping the relationship owned by GHM identities.

Cascade behavior does not grant Saved Business authority to delete either parent resource.

## 13. Indexing

The unique constraint on `(account_id, business_id)` supplies the required uniqueness/index for pair lookup.

An account-first index is also authorized for the primary read/list path:

```sql
CREATE INDEX saved_business_account_created_idx
  ON ghm.saved_business (account_id, created_at DESC);
```

This supports account-owned listing without introducing ranking or recommendation semantics.

## 14. Provider neutrality

The following production-provider mechanisms are deliberately excluded:

- Supabase `auth.uid()`;
- Supabase RLS;
- Supabase Data API grants;
- Supabase client query syntax;
- Realtime;
- Storage;
- provider-specific toggle RPCs.

GHM authentication remains `AuthContext`; authorization remains in the application repository/service boundary plus the database privilege boundary.

## 15. Explicit non-goals

This contract does not authorize:

- Business profile mutation;
- Business verification adjudication;
- Business activation mutation;
- Trust calculation or Trust mutation;
- Business membership mutation;
- saved Business notes, tags, folders, or sharing;
- notifications generated from saving;
- recommendations or ranking;
- analytics events;
- expiry/archival rules;
- administrator saved lists;
- public visibility of a user's saved relationships.

## 16. Qualification gate

Implementation cannot close until runtime qualification independently proves:

1. exact schema and foreign keys;
2. uniqueness of `(account_id, business_id)`;
3. runtime/cleanup identity separation;
4. exact runtime ACL boundary;
5. authenticated account binding;
6. eligible create;
7. duplicate create handling;
8. ineligible create denial;
9. own read/list;
10. cross-account read isolation;
11. own delete;
12. cross-account delete denial;
13. arbitrary UPDATE denial;
14. unauthenticated denial where the exposed boundary permits such a test;
15. persisted relationship reconciliation;
16. governed cleanup without runtime ACL weakening.

## 17. Source reconciliation

The production source audit establishes the Saved Business relation as `public.saved_businesses` with `user_id`, `business_id`, `created_at`, uniqueness on the account/business pair, owner-scoped SELECT/INSERT/DELETE, and eligibility requiring an active, verified, approved Business.

GHM maps those concepts to its existing canonical identity resources:

```text
production user_id   -> ghm.account_identity.id
production business_id -> ghm.business.id
public.saved_businesses -> ghm.saved_business
```

No new account or Business identity model is introduced by Saved Business.
