## Review implementation state — NOT YET RUNTIME QUALIFIED

The physical Review schema is now live in the construction database, but the Review runtime/resource boundary has **not** yet been implemented or qualified.

The first-slice operation vocabulary is:

```text
review.create
review.readOwn
review.readPublic
review.readPending
review.approve
review.reject
```

No `review.delete` operation is authorized.

Required implementation boundary:

- authenticated customer-only creation;
- reviewer identity bound to `AuthContext.userId`;
- reviewer snapshot derived from canonical account identity;
- eligible Business required;
- active Business owner cannot self-review;
- duplicate prevention under concurrency;
- own-read isolation;
- public reads limited to approved Reviews on eligible Businesses;
- admin-only moderation;
- pending-only moderation transitions;
- moderator identity/time bound to authenticated admin;
- rejection reason required for rejection;
- moderation and approved-review aggregate maintenance atomic in one transaction;
- Review/Trust-owned aggregates protected from ordinary Business mutation;
- no runtime delete;
- dedicated `ghm_runtime` least-privilege qualification;
- rollback and concurrency evidence.
