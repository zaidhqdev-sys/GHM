## Source-of-truth reconciliation — CLOSED / PASS

Authoritative Zaid Connect evidence confirms:

- customers author Reviews through `reviews.reviewer_id`;
- Reviews target Businesses through `reviews.business_id`;
- only customer accounts may submit Reviews;
- Business owners cannot review their own Business;
- `businesses.rating` and `businesses.review_count` are protected aggregates owned by the Reviews/Trust capability;
- aggregates are derived from approved Reviews only;
- `rating` is the approved-review average rounded to two decimals;
- `review_count` is the count of approved Reviews;
- no approved Reviews produces `rating = 0` and `review_count = 0`.

Evidence is recorded in `docs/architecture/REVIEW_AGGREGATE_OWNERSHIP_CONTRACT.md`.
